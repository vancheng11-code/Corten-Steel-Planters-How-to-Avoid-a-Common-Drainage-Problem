Can Corten Steel Be Used for Privacy Screens?

Corten steel privacy screens are popular in gardens, patios, commercial landscapes, and architectural spaces.

Laser-cut patterns allow designers to control how much light and visibility pass through the panel.

A dense pattern can provide greater privacy, while larger openings create a more open visual effect.

The screen can be designed as a standalone panel, fence, partition, or decorative architectural element.

Panel dimensions and thickness should be selected according to the installation environment.

Support posts and fixing systems are also important, particularly for tall outdoor screens exposed to wind.

Designers should consider drainage and water runoff as the weathering process develops.

Custom patterns can include geometric designs, natural motifs, logos, or project-specific artwork.
