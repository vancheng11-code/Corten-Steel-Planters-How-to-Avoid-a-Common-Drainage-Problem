
Large outdoor spaces can sometimes feel visually disconnected.

Corten steel can be used to define different zones for seating, planting, pathways, dining, or recreational activities.

Low edging can create boundaries without obstructing views, while taller panels can provide stronger separation.

The warm weathered appearance of Corten steel can also help connect hardscape elements with natural landscaping.

For commercial projects, repeated Corten elements can create a consistent visual language across the site.

The final design should consider dimensions, installation, drainage, and interaction with surrounding materials.
