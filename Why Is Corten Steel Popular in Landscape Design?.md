
Landscape designers often use Corten steel when they want a material that combines a strong industrial appearance with a natural outdoor character.

Its weathered surface develops a distinctive rust-colored patina over time, creating a contrast with green plants, stone, concrete, and timber.

Corten steel is commonly fabricated into garden planters, raised beds, edging, retaining features, decorative screens, and fire pits.

Another advantage is design flexibility. Sheets can be laser cut, bent, welded, and fabricated into custom shapes.

However, the installation environment should be considered carefully. Drainage, water runoff, surrounding surfaces, and structural requirements can all affect the final result.
